## Getting Started

### System Requirements
- A modern web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari).
- An active internet connection.
- Your registered Business Email and Password provided by the administrator.

---

## Login

1. Navigate to the Vendor Portal URL in your web browser.
2. Enter your **Business Email** in the email field.
3. Enter your **Password** in the password field.
4. Click the **Login** button.

> **Note:** If you enter incorrect credentials, an error message will appear. Please ensure your email and password are correct.

---

## Dashboard

After a successful login, you will be directed to the **Dashboard**. This is your central hub for account information and quick navigation.

### Features:
- **Welcome Message:** Displays your contact person's name or vendor name.
- **Profile Information:**
  - **Company Name:** Your registered company name.
  - **Business Email:** The email address associated with your account.
  - **Contact Person:** The primary contact person for your account.
- **Recent Activity:** Shows a log of your recent interactions with the system (if available).

## Managing Orders

### Pending Orders

Navigate to the **Pending Orders** page via the sidebar menu. This section lists all orders that are currently pending.

**Key Actions:**
- **View Orders:** See a list of pending orders with details like Order Number, Date, etc.
- **Sort:** Click on column headers (e.g., Order No) to sort the list in Ascending or Descending order.
- **Pagination:** Use the page numbers at the bottom to navigate through large lists of orders.
- **View Images:** If an order has an associated image, click the image icon/button to view a larger version of the product image along with its description and item code.

### Purchase Order Drafts

Navigate to the **Purchase Order Draft** page via the sidebar menu.

**Key Actions:**
- **Filter by Category:** Use the dropdown menu to filter drafts by category:
  - Material
  - Preps
  - Accessories
  - Packaging
  - Finish Product
- **View Details:** Browse the list of draft orders for the selected category.
- **View Images:** Similar to pending orders, you can click on available images to view product details.

## Account Management

### Change Password

It is recommended to update your password periodically for security.

1. Navigate to the **Change Password** page.
2. Enter your **Old Password**.
3. Enter your **New Password**.
4. **Confirm New Password** by re-entering it.
5. Click **Update Password**.

**Password Requirements:**
- Minimum **8 characters** long.
- Must contain at least one **uppercase letter** (A-Z).
- Must contain at least one **lowercase letter** (a-z).
- Must contain at least one **number** (0-9).
- Must contain at least one **special character** (@, $, !, %, *, ?, &).

> **Important:** The new password cannot be the same as your old password.

## Troubleshooting

### Login Failed
- **Invalid Email or Password:** Double-check your credentials. Ensure Caps Lock is off.
- **Network Error:** Check your internet connection.

### No Data Visible
- If you see no orders in "Pending Orders" or "Purchase Order Drafts," it means there are currently no records associated with your account for the selected criteria.

### Images Not Loading
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
- If an image icon is not clickable or shows a broken link, the image might not be available on the server.
